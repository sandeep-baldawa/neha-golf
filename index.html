RESEARCH & ACTIVITIES

Sports medicine, from both sides

Neha is on a pre-med track aimed at sports medicine, and her independent research is about the sport she plays: an ISEF project on fatigue-induced neuromuscular instability in adolescent golfers, developed with two university biomechanics faculty advisors. She was accepted to the ASDRP summer research program for 2026.

Outside of golf and the lab, she co-leads Muzic4Lives, a nonprofit that brings live music into senior communities, dances Kathak, and is working toward her second-degree Kukkiwon black belt in taekwondo after earning her first degree in September 2024.
